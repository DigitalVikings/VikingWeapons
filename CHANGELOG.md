# Changelog

All notable changes to **VikingWeapons** will be documented in this file.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- **1H Spear Overhaul**: Spears are gripped forward for natural underhand thrusts, +0.6m forward reach, and lowered hitboxes to strike small ground prey reliably.
- **Hamstring Throw Slow**: Spear throwing attacks inflict a 30% movement slow on hit targets for 6 seconds.
- **Tactical Secondary Conversions**: Distinct functional damage profiles for heavy attacks: Piercing sword thrusts, Blunt battleaxe pommel pokes, Slashing atgeir cleaves, and Piercing knife leap attacks.
- **Knife Archetype Overhaul**: Horizontal primary combo swings convert to pure Slashing damage, while combo finishers and leap stabs deliver high-damage Piercing strikes.
- **Primary Weapon Cleave Profiles**: Multi-target cleave on sword and battleaxe swings through VikingLib's cleave engine without split damage penalties.
- **Responsive Attack Cadence**: Accelerated secondary windup animations on 1H Axes and Maces for fluid, competitive combat pacing.
